# Natural LOWTOWN archipelago

The city used one repeating row/column layout and slightly perturbed rectangular shorelines. Each of the 16 major islands now has a separate authored coast with capes, bays and elongated headlands. Twelve town parcels are staggered, while their streets, buildings, facilities and residents move together. The first coastal belt retains its existing civic/service anchors. The world is 18,500 × 19,800 units; four satellite islands form small groups beside existing islands, for 11 minor islands in total.

There are still 24 logical motor connections. Staggered landings use 66 overlapping bridge deck segments, with open rails at corner joints and approach roads only at actual landfalls. Twenty-two boardwalk pieces connect all minor islands, including the satellite chains. Coastal woodland, bluffs, marsh and rock features are placed at authored anchors; foliage clears roads, buildings and walking links. Natural shores use sand and shallows without a continuous artificial kerb. District names follow the actual coastline, including headlands and small islands.

Ordinary traffic follows closed circuits on the connected road graph, with separate lane targets and junction approaches. Turning actors align before accelerating. Opposing lanes use physical chassis clearance rather than the wider following buffer. A responder displaced onto a clear shoulder plans a checked return to the street. A returning responder ahead of a fire engine can clear its nose into the other lane and travel beyond the leader before merging. Traffic facing an occupied yielding shoulder clears the parked car along a checked lane before pulling over. At a blocked responder turn, crossing traffic pulls away from the responder and holds its clearance until the crossing unit has passed. Captured wagon/ambulance and occupied bus shoulder cases are mandatory regressions. The private drive smoke route resolves its eight destinations through the current road graph, retaining geometry, sliding and death/release assertions.

Saves use worldVersion 3. Version 2 locations move with their town parcel; earlier locations use the original-to-current map. Cash, found parts and walking mode remain preserved.

Validation includes exact polygon-to-segment sea gaps and overlap checks, complete road connectivity, clear props/rails/paint, every boardwalk, safe fleet/runways and save migration. The actual controller completed 318 drives in both directions over all 159 streets and motor deck segments, about 498,717 units, including the final vegetation and satellite layout. Populated traffic, custody and responders have separate acceptance tests and browser scenarios. The in-game AutoTest button is not used. The expanded world smoke test checks all 48 actual motor landfalls, overlap at each intermediate deck joint, approach roads, and full vehicle footprints sampled every 12 units on every live traffic circuit. Final reports identify the exact tested revision.
